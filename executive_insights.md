## AI-Assisted Findings

2. **Profitability:** Electronics has the strongest category margin.
3. **Channel:** Organic Search is the top revenue-generating channel.
4. **Region:** South leads regional revenue.
5. **Management focus:** prioritize high-margin categories and evaluate whether high-revenue channels also produce healthy margins.

## Recommended Business Actions
- Protect high-margin categories from excessive discounting.
- Compare channel-level revenue against profitability before increasing acquisition spend.
- Investigate regional differences in order volume and profit.
- Build customer-level repeat-purchase and retention metrics as the next analytical layer.
- Add a predictive churn/retention model in the next portfolio project.
